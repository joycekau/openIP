Guardrail

Before we promote any per-film raise

Compliance gate — "investment per movie"

Promising profit or returns from others' effort can cross into securities territory (the Howey line). openIP's own house rule already reflects this — we speak in terms of revenue → utility / licensing, never dividends, guaranteed returns, or "invest and profit." Get counsel to bless the structure (film-financing SOW, sponsorship, pre-sale, or licensing) and the exact marketing language before any raise. This is a hard gate (Q3-M6 → Q4-M5).
